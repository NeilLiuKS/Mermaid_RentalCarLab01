# 車輛租用系統 Sequence Diagram

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor User as 租車用戶
    participant Account as Account
    participant RentalSystem as RentalSystem
    participant CarType as CarType
    participant RentalTime as RentalTime
    participant FeeCalculator as FeeCalculator
    
    User->>Account: RegisterAccount()
    Note right of Account: 註冊帳號
    Account-->>User: Account Created
    
    User->>RentalSystem: ToRentalCar()
    Note right of RentalSystem: 租用車輛
    
    RentalSystem->>CarType: SelectCarType()
    Note right of CarType: 選擇車型
    CarType-->>RentalSystem: Car Type Selected
    
    RentalSystem->>RentalTime: SelectRentalTime()
    Note right of RentalTime: 選擇租用時間
    RentalTime-->>RentalSystem: Rental Time Selected
    
    RentalSystem->>FeeCalculator: CalculateFee()
    Note right of FeeCalculator: 計算租金
    FeeCalculator-->>RentalSystem: Fee Calculated
    
    RentalSystem-->>User: Rental Confirmed
